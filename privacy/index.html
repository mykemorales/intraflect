Privacy policy

Beta version · Effective [date]

Intraflect is a place to hear yourself. What you say here is personal, so this page explains plainly what we keep, where it goes, and how you remove it.

The short version

  • Your recordings, words and photos are private to you. We don't sell them, show them to anyone, or use them for advertising.
  • Your voice recording and its transcript are sent to OpenAI only to write your transcript and reflection. OpenAI doesn't use them to train its models.
  • Your photos never go to an AI service and are never analysed.
  • You can delete any entry, or everything, from inside the app at any time.

Who is responsible

Intraflect is made by [your full name], [business address], Czech Republic, Business ID (IČO) [IČO]. That's who decides how your data is used (the "controller" under GDPR). You can reach us at hello@intraflect.app.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Voice recordingsSo you can play back your check-ins and so they can be transcribed.
TranscriptsTo show your words and to write the reflection and the question for tomorrow.
Reflections and questionsTo show them to you, and to build your weekly reflection.
Photos (optional)To show yesterday's photo at your next check-in. Skippable, and you can turn it off.
Account IDAn anonymous identifier created on first launch, so your entries stay yours. We don't ask for your name or email in the app.
Crash reportsTechnical details when the app crashes (device model, iOS version, the error), so we can fix it. They don't contain your recordings or words.

We don't use advertising or tracking tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase stores your recordings, photos and entries in private storage, in [region, e.g. the EU (Frankfurt)]. Only your account can access them.
  • OpenAI transcribes your recording and writes your reflection. It doesn't keep the audio after transcribing it. The transcript sent for the reflection may be kept by OpenAI for up to 30 days to detect misuse, then deleted. OpenAI doesn't use either to train its models.
  • Sentry receives crash reports.
  • Apple TestFlight handles beta installation and any feedback you send through it, under Apple's own privacy policy.

OpenAI and Sentry are based in the United States. Transfers there are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Why we're allowed to use it

We process your data to provide the app you asked for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Because what you say can touch on your health or other sensitive parts of your life, we also ask for your explicit consent before your first check-in (Article 9(2)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.

How long we keep it

Your entries stay until you delete them. When you delete an entry or your account, it's removed from the app straight away and from our backups within 30 days.

When the beta ends, we'll tell you whether your data can carry over to the public version. If you don't want it to, we'll delete it.

Your rights

  • Delete any entry, recording or photo, or your whole account, in the app under You → Privacy & data.
  • Export everything (audio, photos, transcripts and reflections) from the same place.
  • Access, correct or restrict your data, or object to how we use it, by writing to us.

If you think we've handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ) at uoou.gov.cz, or to the data protection authority where you live.

What Intraflect is not

Intraflect is a reflection tool. It isn't therapy, medical advice or a crisis service. If you're struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a professional. Intraflect is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app before the change applies to you.
