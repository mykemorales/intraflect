Journaling asks you to write. Most of us think out loud.

Blank pages, prompts and streaks turn reflection into homework. So most journals end up abandoned by February.

Intraflect is quieter than that. You talk for two to four minutes, the way you'd talk to a friend on a walk. No typing, no performing, nothing to keep up.

What you get back is short and specific: what you actually said, noticed, and one question worth sleeping on. You stay the main character. The AI just helps you listen.

Speak. Reflect. Return.

Three steps, a few minutes a day, whenever you feel like it. Skip a day and nothing breaks.

Made for people who want to understand themselves, not optimise themselves.

You've started journals before

Quiet on purpose.

Intraflect leaves out the things that turn reflection into a chore.

  • StreaksSkip a day, a week, a month. You're always welcome back.
  • A chatbotNo conversation to keep up. You speak, it reflects once, and you're done.
  • AdviceIt reflects what you said and asks rather than assumes. It never tells you what to do.
  • Mood scoresNo charts rating your feelings. Just your own words, over time.
  • Face analysisYour photo is a record of you on that day. It's never analysed.

Saved and private

What you say here stays yours.

  • Your recordings, words and photos are private. They're never sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Audio is sent to OpenAI only to transcribe it and write your reflection, and it isn't used to train AI models.
  • Photos never leave for any AI service.
  • Export everything, or delete any entry or your whole account, from inside the app.
  • Read the full privacy policy
